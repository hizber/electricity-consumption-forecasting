# Assignment requirements

**Source:** [[Group Work assignment-2026.pdf]], pages 1–3. This is a summary of the supplied PDF, verified byte-for-byte against the live Moodle assignment on 6 November 2026. Refer to the PDF for exact wording.

## Objective
- Develop a neural-network model that predicts the next hour of electricity consumption in a real house as accurately as possible.
- Extra: predict the next day, 24 hours of consumption.

## Group work
- Form groups of 4–5 students, assign roles, make a project schedule, and schedule meetings at least weekly.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and prepare the dataset. Consider enrichment, day of week, feature engineering, weather information, and relevant features.
- Each student selects a time-series neural-network algorithm to study, documents their chosen network and produces their own implementation. The examples include RNN, LSTM, GRU, TCN, Transformer and WaveNet.
- Each student records how AI was used and shares their implementation with the group. The PDF calls for multiple solutions and implementations.

## Deliverables
One group document, using the Jamk thesis template and project reporting instructions, with references in Jamk APA 7. The PDF calls for:
- A time-series neural-network prestudy and a short EDA summary.
- Reasons for the selected neural networks, each student's implementation, and network structures. Code snippets may be included.
- Results collected in one table and explained.
- Conclusions addressing large-error days, possible explanations and whether accuracy is the same across test and all three evaluation sets.
- Jupyter notebook HTML files as attachments.

[Jamk project reporting instructions](https://help.jamk.fi/raportointiohje/en/) — linked by the assignment; includes reporting templates.

## Grading
The PDF gives group-work evaluation by Mika as 0–20 and peer and self-evaluations as 0–5. Its formula is:

`points = (mean peer evaluation / 5) × (self-evaluation / 5) × group-work evaluation`
